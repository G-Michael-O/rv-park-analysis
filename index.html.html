How to use this register

  • Priority review: start with records where both price and NOI are explicitly reported and the calculated cap rate meets or exceeds 9.5%, then confirm current status and request a current trailing-12-month P&L and rent roll.
  • Mixed-use assets: RV parks with cabins, mobile homes, motel rooms, storage, marina, retail or other businesses should not be presented as RV-only cap rates unless the income and expenses are segregated.
  • Conflicts: when the headline cap rate differs from NOI ÷ asking price, retain both figures and resolve with the broker; do not silently pick the higher rate.
  • Inactive or uncertain listings: off-market, expired, pending, stale or status-conflicted assets are retained only as research leads, not current acquisition opportunities.
  • Missing metrics: “not disclosed” means the public source did not provide a reliable figure in the source capture. Do not back-solve NOI from an advertised cap rate and present it as reported NOI.
Prepared as a source-navigation and screening aid, not an appraisal, investment recommendation, or independent audit. Verify current asking price, status, TTM financial statements, rent roll, taxes, insurance, utilities, repairs/capex, land tenure, permits and title directly with the listing broker and public records before relying on any metric. Some source links are search pages where a direct original listing URL was not preserved in the prior research; these are labelled as such.
